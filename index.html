Polo Studios

Good taste.
New ideas.

Polo Studios is an independent technology studio based in Puerto Rico, founded by Miguel, a computer engineering student with a focus on software development, web applications, and artificial intelligence. The studio brings together technical learning, creative interests, and a drive to build useful digital products.

Our ideas start with curiosity: how can technology make a task easier, help someone discover something new, or turn a personal interest into an experience worth sharing? Through projects in web development, computer vision, and embedded systems, we explore those questions and develop the skills to bring concepts to life.

Fragrances, sneakers, hoodies, and gaming influence the studio’s perspective—from its visual identity to future product ideas. A planned fragrance discovery platform is one example of that connection. Rooted in Puerto Rico, Polo Studios aims to contribute to the island’s technology community while creating products that can reach people anywhere.
