Table rules & payouts

One shuffled 52-card deck per hand. Dealer checks for blackjack and stands on all 17s, including soft 17. Natural blackjack pays 3:2; other wins pay 1:1; ties return your bet. Bets use even credits to keep 3:2 payouts in whole credits. Hit to 21 automatically stands. No splitting, doubling, insurance, or surrender. Your current hand is saved if you refresh.
